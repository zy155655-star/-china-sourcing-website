What kinds of products do you source?

We can help with many categories including home goods, consumer products, packaging, accessories and other general products. For regulated or highly specialized products, we'll first determine whether the project is suitable.

Do I need to already have a supplier?

No. You can bring a product idea, competitor link, photo, specification or existing supplier information.

How do you charge for your service?

Pricing depends on the sourcing work required. We'll discuss the scope and agree on the service fee before starting.

Can you help with shipping?

We can help coordinate shipping information and connect the order with suitable logistics providers. Final freight and customs arrangements depend on the product and destination.
