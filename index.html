What kinds of products can you help source?

We can help explore many general product categories, including home goods, consumer products, packaging, accessories and other products. If your project is regulated, highly specialized or otherwise complex, we'll first determine whether it is suitable for our sourcing service.

Do I need to already have a supplier?

No. You can start with a product idea, photo, competitor link, specification, existing supplier or even a simple description of what you are looking for.

Do you only work with large companies?

No. We can discuss sourcing requests from businesses of different sizes. The right approach depends on your product, quantity, budget and requirements.

How do you charge for your service?

Pricing depends on the sourcing work required. We'll discuss the scope and agree on the service fee before starting.

Can you help with samples and quality inspection?

We can help coordinate samples and quality inspection arrangements when required. The exact inspection method and cost depend on the product and order.

Can you help with shipping?

We can help coordinate shipping information and connect the order with suitable logistics providers. Final freight, customs and import arrangements depend on the product and destination.
